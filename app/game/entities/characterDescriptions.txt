Tanky Character: 
Tanky character with high hp and strong focus shot but slow movement speed

        Focus shot: 28 dps
            7 Bullets tight spread 
                3 aimed straight up, 
                4 aimed inwards at slight angles

        Unfocused shot: 
            3 bullets, 
                1 aimed straight up, 
                2 at wide angles

        firerate: 0.25 seconds

    Stats:
        Max hp: 6
        Starting hp: 3
        hitIframesDuration: 2 seconds

        Focus Speed: 50 pixels per second
        Unfocused Speed: 110 pixels per second

    Special Mechanics:
        None, straightforward character with high hp and strong focus shot but slow movement speed
